Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Welcome to Nova News. This page explains what information this website does and does not collect.

News content

Nova News shows headlines and short summaries collected automatically from trusted public news sources (such as the BBC, Al Jazeera, NHK and Dawn) in five languages, with live cryptocurrency prices from CoinGecko. Tapping "Read full story" opens the original publisher’s website. This site does not copy or republish full articles.

What is counted

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. This site has no sign-up and no login.

Advertising

This website may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings (google.com/settings/ads).

External links

News links open the original publishers' websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.
